## Historical qualitative-methods sources

- `source_pages/dubois_seventh_ward_map.jpg`: Du Bois's 1899 map of Philadelphia's Seventh Ward. Public-domain Harvard scan obtained through Wikimedia Commons.
- `source_pages/dubois_methods_crop.png` and `source_pages/dubois_schedule_crop.png`: crops from the 1899 edition of *The Philadelphia Negro*, pp. 1 and 402.
- `source_pages/polish_peasant_method_crop.png` and `source_pages/polish_peasant_life_record.png`: crops from Thomas and Znaniecki's 1919 *The Polish Peasant in Europe and America*, volume 3, pp. 6 and 97.
- `source_pages/whyte_north_end_tenements_1930s.jpg`: Boston Public Library photograph of North End tenements, approximately 1930–1939, file 08_02_002080. It documents the neighbourhood and period, not Whyte's own fieldwork.
- `source_pages/whyte_street_corner_society_cover.jpg`: official University of Chicago Press cover image for the fourth edition of William Foote Whyte's *Street Corner Society*, ISBN 9780226895451.
- `source_pages/emerson_jottings_crop.png`: published example of ethnographic jottings from Emerson, Fretz and Shaw's *Writing Ethnographic Fieldnotes*, 2nd ed., p. 30.
- `source_pages/merton_focused_abstract_crop.png`: title and abstract of Merton and Kendall's 1946 “The Focused Interview.”

The corresponding public-domain or instructor-held source PDFs are stored under `literature/source_pdfs/session03/`. Crops retain the original wording and typography; explanatory text and citations remain outside the images.
