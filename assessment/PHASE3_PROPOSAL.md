# Assessment design — approved structure

**Status:** Revised and approved on 6 November 2026.

## Assessment weights

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Weekly coding exercises | 20% |
| Midterm project proposal | 20% |
| Final research-design memo | 20% |
| Project presentation | 30% |
| Participation | 10% |

These five components make up the entire course grade.

## Weekly coding exercises

The ten best weekly submissions count. Students submit a narrated screen recording that runs the supplied exercise, explains the input and output of each operation, changes one named input or parameter, and states what the result does and does not show. Weekly work receives completion marks rather than numerical quality grades. Missing elements may be supplied within one week.

## Midterm project proposal

The 1,200–1,500 word proposal is the first substantial version of the final project. It sets out the sociological question, focused literature position, proposed evidence and design, role of the LLM, one worked input/output/check example, validation and failure analysis, ethical issues, reproducibility record and next steps. It is not a Python test or a completed empirical study.

## Final research-design memo

The final memo develops the proposal into a complete design for an LLM-related sociological study. It includes the sociological motivation, literature position, inferential target, data and sampling plan, proposed LLM workflow, prototype or trace where feasible, validation strategy, failure modes and ethics, reproducibility record, AI-use disclosure, and a short account of revisions since the midterm.

The memo is worth 20% and must be submitted before the student's individually scheduled meeting with the instructor on 2 December. It is the final written submission.

## Project presentation

On 9 December, each student gives a 10–12 minute presentation followed by questions. The presentation is worth 30%. It should make the sociological problem, proposed evidence, role of the LLM, validation strategy and most important limitation clear. Assessment considers the clarity and coherence of the argument, effective use of evidence or an inspectable trace, and the ability to explain and defend the design in discussion.

## Participation

Participation is worth 10%. It may take the form of a reading question, explanation of a code block, validation critique, project feedback or a short written contribution. Speaking frequency alone does not determine the mark.

## AI use

AI use is permitted with responsibility and disclosure. Students remain responsible for every submitted claim and line of code. Material assistance requires a brief disclosure naming the tool or model, its purpose, what was incorporated and how it was checked. When an LLM is part of the research method, the research record should preserve prompts, outputs, settings, dates, provider or local runtime, transformations and validation decisions.

## Access and environment

- Grade reasoning and interpretation rather than access to expensive hardware or models.
- Assessed coding exercises must have a recorded-output fallback.
- Hosted calls use the shared, capped OpenRouter key; local execution remains available through Ollama.
- Identifiable or sensitive project data may not be sent through the shared key.
