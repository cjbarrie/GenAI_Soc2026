# Candidate Visual Directions

**Status:** Direction A — Evidence & Society selected on 2026-08-18. The exact system is recorded in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

All three directions assume 16:9 Quarto/Reveal.js slides, projection-first legibility, web and PDF export, one idea per slide, direct chart labeling, small but readable citations, and a separate workbook for extended code. None uses stock “AI brain,” robot, neon circuit, or chat-bubble imagery as a default visual language.

## Direction A — Evidence & Society (selected)

An editorial academic system: serious without looking institutional, contemporary without looking like a technology pitch deck.

- **Mood:** sociological journal, annotated evidence, field notes, and clean data graphics.
- **Palette:** warm paper (`#F5F1E8`), near-black ink (`#182026`), deep cobalt (`#2457A6`), vermilion (`#D5573B`), muted moss (`#6D8061`).
- **Typography:** Source Serif 4 for major questions and section openings; Source Sans 3 for explanatory text; IBM Plex Mono for code and research records.
- **Composition:** asymmetric editorial grid; generous margins; figures occupy the canvas rather than sitting inside cards; thin rules and margin annotations provide structure.
- **Charts:** ink/cobalt base, vermilion for the focal comparison, moss for validation or accepted evidence; direct labels instead of legends where possible.
- **Images:** tightly cropped documentary/research imagery, archival material when substantively relevant, and clean reconstructions of paper figures.
- **Code:** warm-gray code field with one highlighted line and an adjacent plain-language consequence—not a terminal aesthetic.
- **Best feature:** gives sociology and computation equal visual status and exports well to light-background PDFs.
- **Risk:** serif display type and warm backgrounds need disciplined spacing to avoid looking literary or nostalgic.

## Direction B — The Research Instrument

A crisp, methods-forward system organized around observable inputs, transformations, outputs, and checks.

- **Mood:** transparent apparatus, measurement protocol, and modern methods laboratory.
- **Palette:** chalk white (`#F8FAFC`), graphite (`#16212A`), strong blue (`#1769AA`), teal (`#168C8C`), signal amber (`#E59A2F`).
- **Typography:** Atkinson Hyperlegible or IBM Plex Sans throughout; IBM Plex Mono for code.
- **Composition:** stable left alignment, visible baseline grid, large numerical or conceptual anchors, sparse protocol lines, and occasional edge-to-edge figures.
- **Charts:** restrained blue/graphite system; amber reserved for uncertainty or failure; uncertainty intervals and human/LLM distinctions remain visible in grayscale.
- **Images:** primarily empirical—study diagrams, data, maps, interfaces only when necessary—with minimal decorative photography.
- **Code:** syntax-light blocks paired with labeled input and output objects; state changes use alignment rather than boxes and arrows.
- **Best feature:** maximizes accessibility and makes recurring validation practices instantly recognizable.
- **Risk:** without editorial imagery and varied silhouettes, it could feel like a polished methods workshop rather than a sociology seminar.

## Direction C — Night Seminar

A dark, high-contrast projection system designed for discussion, tension, and strong empirical reveals.

- **Mood:** evening seminar, model observatory, contested evidence.
- **Palette:** blue-black (`#111820`), soft ivory (`#F1EEE6`), electric cyan (`#49BBD1`), coral (`#F06A5B`), muted lavender (`#9A8FC5`).
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk or IBM Plex Sans for display and body; IBM Plex Mono for code.
- **Composition:** large question-led titles, strong photographic or plotted focal points, sparse copy, occasional split-screen comparisons.
- **Charts:** luminous lines and points on dark ground; coral marks contradiction or failure; labels sit directly next to data.
- **Images:** high-contrast documentary crops, satellite/street imagery, interaction sequences, and model-output comparisons.
- **Code:** near-black-on-black is avoided; code uses a slightly lighter slate field and large type.
- **Best feature:** strongest classroom presence and especially effective for multimodal evidence, simulation, and audit sessions.
- **Risk:** weakest for printing, bright classrooms, long code explanations, and accessibility if contrast is not checked rigorously.

## Selection rationale

**Direction A — Evidence & Society** was selected. It best fits a course that is sociological first but methodologically exacting, and it can accommodate qualitative excerpts, experimental designs, code, networks, multimodal evidence, and audit plots without making any one of those the default identity of the course.

Direction B is the best choice if maximum clarity and repeatable methods cues matter more than disciplinary atmosphere. Direction C should be chosen only if classroom projection is decisively more important than printed/PDF use.

After selection, `design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` will specify exact typography, sizing, margins, grid, spacing, components, chart semantics, citations, source notes, accessibility, and rendering tests. The first implementation will remain the single pilot week required by Phase 5.
